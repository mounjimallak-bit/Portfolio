Axiora Data: socio-economic analytics platform

Morocco's national statistics institute publishes its data as PDFs and spreadsheets, which makes comparison across regions slow. Axiora brings a hand-transcribed set of those tables, checked against their own totals, into an interactive platform covering six domains: population, labour, housing, finance, education and poverty.

For the Souss-Massa region, it renders choropleth maps at provincial and commune level from official boundary shapefiles, and generates formatted PDF reports from uploaded Excel files. A conversational module built on the Gemini API answers questions about the loaded indicators in plain language.
